ART STYLE (follow exactly): minimalist flat vector anime illustration in the manner of a screen-printed poster, identical in technique to the reference picture(s). It will be cut out and composited into a motion-graphics music video.
- A strictly limited palette, given above. No other colours at all: no extra tints, no skin pink, no grey, no gold.
- No outlines, no lineart, no gradients, no texture, no soft shading. Every form is built from flat, hard-edged colour shapes. Shadows are solid dark shapes; highlights are cream negative space.
- Hair is drawn as a few big, sweeping, flat strands with sharp tapered tips. The face is simple and elegant, with small flat eyes and a few sharp shapes. Hands are clean and correctly drawn, with five fingers each.
BACKGROUND (very important): a perfectly flat, solid pure green (#00FF00) fill from edge to edge. No floor, no water, no circle, no frame, no lettering, no symbol, no ground shadow, no gradient, no particles, no signature. Do not use any green on the character. Wherever the figure is not deliberately cut off by an image edge (as the framing above says), keep clear green margin around it, including every hair tip, so it can be keyed out cleanly.
