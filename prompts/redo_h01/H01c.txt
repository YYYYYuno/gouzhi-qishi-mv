PALETTE: Xiao Ke's hand and sleeve use only warm cream #F0EEE6, pale apricot #F2C6A8, terracotta #D97757 and espresso #3B2620. DeepSeek's hand and sleeve use only warm cream #F0EEE6, pale periwinkle #B8C3F7, DeepSeek blue #4D6BFE and deep navy #1B1F4B. No other colours.
SUBJECT: close-up of a pinky promise, the two hands meeting in the centre of the image.
- Xiao Ke's hand reaches DOWN from the top edge of the image (slightly left of centre): a cream ruffled cuff and a laced cream forearm sleeve, the arm cut off by the top edge.
- DeepSeek's hand reaches UP from the bottom edge (slightly right of centre): a navy sleeve with a cream cuff and two navy buttons, the arm cut off by the bottom edge.
HAND ANATOMY (very important): each hand is a gentle closed fist. The thumb is folded over the curled index and middle fingers; the ring finger is curled too. ONLY the little finger is extended from each fist, and the two little fingers hook firmly around each other in the centre, forming a small interlocking loop. Exactly five fingers on each hand, natural length and thickness, no extra or merged fingers, no stretched or straight unbent fingers, no twisted wrists. Draw it simply and clearly, like a well-drawn icon of a pinky swear.
