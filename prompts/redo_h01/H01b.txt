PALETTE: Xiao Ke's hand and sleeve use only warm cream #F0EEE6, pale apricot #F2C6A8, terracotta #D97757 and espresso #3B2620. DeepSeek's hand and sleeve use only warm cream #F0EEE6, pale periwinkle #B8C3F7, DeepSeek blue #4D6BFE and deep navy #1B1F4B. No other colours.
SUBJECT: side view of a pinky swear between two people facing each other, hands meeting in the middle of the image at the same height.
- On the LEFT: Xiao Ke's hand, coming in horizontally from the left edge, a cream ruffled cuff with a laced cream sleeve, cut off by the left edge.
- On the RIGHT: DeepSeek's hand, coming in horizontally from the right edge, a navy sleeve with a cream cuff and two navy buttons, cut off by the right edge.
HAND ANATOMY (very important): both hands are loosely closed fists seen from the side, knuckles up. In each hand the thumb lies on top of the curled index finger; index, middle and ring fingers are curled under. ONLY the little fingers are extended toward each other and hook together in the centre, one little finger looping over the other. Exactly five fingers per hand, natural proportions, no extra, missing or merged fingers, no bent-back joints. Clean, simple shapes.
