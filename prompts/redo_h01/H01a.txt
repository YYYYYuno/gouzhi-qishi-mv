SUBJECT: a classic pinky promise, two hands, centred, simple and anatomically correct.
- Xiao Ke's right hand comes in from the TOP-LEFT of the image, wrist and cuff near the top-left edge, the back of the hand toward the viewer. Her cream ruffled cuff and laced cream forearm sleeve are visible; the arm is cut off by the image edge.
- DeepSeek's right hand comes in from the BOTTOM-RIGHT, wrist and cuff near the bottom-right edge, palm side toward the viewer. Her navy sleeve with a cream cuff and two navy buttons is visible; the arm is cut off by the image edge.
HAND ANATOMY (very important): each hand is a soft, relaxed fist. In each hand the thumb rests along the side of the curled index finger; the index, middle and ring fingers are curled into the palm and tucked, so only their bent knuckles show. ONLY the little finger of each hand is extended, and it bends into a small hook. The two little fingers hook around each other in the exact centre of the image. Each hand has exactly five fingers (thumb, index, middle, ring, little), each finger a normal length and thickness, no extra or merged fingers, no twisted joints. Keep the shapes few and clean, like a simple, well-drawn pinky-swear icon.
