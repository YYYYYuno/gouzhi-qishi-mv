ART STYLE (style B, follow exactly): a flat graphic anime illustration in the manner of a screen-printed poster, made to be cut out and composited into a motion-graphics music video.
- NO lineart at all: every shape is defined only by flat areas of colour with crisp hard edges. Each colour family uses at most 3-4 flat tones (light, base, shadow, deep shadow). Shadows are bold graphic shapes. No gradients, no airbrush, no glow, no texture, no outlines.
- Palette: everything is neutral (warm off-white, light grey, mid grey, charcoal, near-black) EXCEPT the character's own accent colour family, given in the character description. That accent family is the only saturated colour in the image.
- Simplified but elegant anime face: small sharp highlights in the eyes, clean hair shapes with a few long flowing strands.
BACKGROUND (very important): a perfectly flat, solid pure green (#00FF00) fill from edge to edge. No floor, no ground shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no particles, no frame, no text, no signature. Do not use any green on the character. The whole figure, including every hair tip, fits inside the image with clear green margin on all sides so it can be keyed out cleanly.
